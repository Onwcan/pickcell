End-to-end safety stop: safeedge decision -> pickcell stops commanding
======================================================================

Two processes. safeedge stamps the instant it entered the safe state on
/safety; the cell subtracts that from its own observation. Poll interval
100 ms, cell control period 1 ms, 10 trips.

The bound is the poll interval, as scripts/measure-reaction.sh predicts.
Nothing here is a property of the safety runtime: safeedge decides in
about a millisecond and the cell reacts within one control period of
learning. The interval is the link.
